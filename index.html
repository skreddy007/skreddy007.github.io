Internal

Vulnerability posture dashboard (internal, Nutanix)

Internal tool, details generalized

Jr. Full Stack Developer (contract) via Amiseq and Mastech Digital, client Nutanix.

Problem
Scanner data was scattered everywhere, and vulnerability data was tracked in weekly and monthly spreadsheets.
What I built
An internal dashboard that unifies scanner data from Wiz, Harbor, and Nessus into a single view. Stack: Python.
What it shows
Each product's current vulnerability posture, plus weekly and quarterly vulnerability-management posture per product. SLA metrics include SLA attainment, burndown ratio, and MTTR. Product teams use it to see the security posture of their own products.
Outcome
It replaced the weekly and monthly vulnerability spreadsheets and made it easy for product teams to look into the security side of their product.
